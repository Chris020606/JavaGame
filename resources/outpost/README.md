# Outpost assets

`raider.glb`, `archer.glb`, and `shaman.glb` are derived from the bundled KayKit Adventurers Rogue_Hooded, Ranger, and Mage. Movement/melee clips come from `resources/knight.glb`; bow and spellcasting clips come from the retained KayKit source `resources/source/Rig_Medium_CombatRanged.glb`. KayKit character and animation packs are CC0; see the License.txt files in their respective resource folders. The sword remains in the original Adventurers Assets/gltf directory with its texture and binary buffer.

The scenery OBJ/MTL files are original geometry created for this project. Rebuild all derived assets from the project root with `python3 tools/prepare_assets.py`. Do not move the derived raider independently of its referenced textures.

`hero_archer.glb` and `hero_mage.glb` use the Ranger and Mage meshes, the full knight movement/melee animation set, and class-specific ranged clips from the same bundled CC0 sources. Their textures remain relative to the Adventurers character directory.

The `nature/` directory contains selected unmodified Kenney Nature Kit (2.1) OBJ/MTLs downloaded from https://opengameart.org/content/nature-kit under CC0. The original license and source/archive checksum are included. To reimport, run `python3 tools/import_nature.py /path/to/Nature-Kit.zip`. These downloaded models are independent of `prepare_assets.py`.

The `bosses/` directory includes pinned CC0 KayKit Skeletons models, embedded animations, weapon accessories, license, and source/checksum manifest. `Hollow_King.glb` derives from Skeleton_Mage by removing its hat mesh (including the unreferenced mesh entry). The animated rig is preserved; the crown and eye glow are drawn by the game. Reproduce the download and variant using `python3 tools/import_bosses.py`.

`daylight.vs/fs` implement the wind-deformed material pass with GGX/Schlick lighting, hemisphere ambient light, local light sources, distance/height fog, and nine-tap PCF shadows. `shadow.fs` writes RGB-packed depth with opaque alpha. `post.fs` applies FXAA, highlight bloom, ACES tone mapping, and gamma correction to the scene; the UI is composited separately. High uses a 2048 shadow atlas and up to 1920 scene pixels across, Balanced uses 1024 and 1440. Material-map BRDF slot 10 is reserved for the shadow atlas; these assets do not contain BRDF textures.

`graveyard/` contains 22 selected Kenney Graveyard Kit 5.0 OBJ/MTLs and their shared texture atlas, CC0 license and archive checksum. Reimport with `python3 tools/import_graveyard.py /path/to/kenney_graveyard-kit_5.0.zip`. The cemetery and necropolis use these models at runtime; the pass uses Nature Kit bridge and cliff meshes. Chapter recoloring preserves the original source material colors between switches.

`equipment/` contains class-fitted, skinned armor for Warrior, Archer, and Mage (three tiers × four slots × three classes), generated by `python3 tools/fit_armor.py` from the bundled CC0 KayKit surfaces. Source skin weights/skeletons are preserved; covered original surfaces are masked while equipped, and each piece receives the exact rendered player pose. Shared vertices receive consistent normal clearance to avoid cracks. Ranger helmets are open-faced; Mage armor retains a fitted wizard hat. Original Dawnblade/Dawnstaff OBJ meshes are generated separately by `python3 tools/create_equipment.py`. Rigid box armor has been removed. `preview.fs` supplies studio lighting for independent inventory and hero-selection render targets.

`effects/` contains original soft particle, spark, ring, and rune textures generated by `python3 tools/create_effects.py`. CombatVfx renders them additively with depth testing and no depth writes, alongside tapered weapon ribbons and arrow meshes. It preserves gameplay hit areas and telegraph timing. EnvironmentLayout contains authored prop groupings and seeded horizon groves shared by the lit and shadow passes.
