### Warrior

Break a raider’s guard with a heavy strike, then parry the counterattack. Find **Goran the Iron Hand** in Aethelgard for weapons and armor.

![Warrior weapons and all twelve armor pieces](docs/atlas/warrior-collection.jpg)

### Archer

Keep moving between shots. Trade your bow for a hand crossbow or the heavier siege crossbow. Find **Diana the Huntress** in Aethelgard for weapons and armor.

![Archer weapons and all twelve armor pieces](docs/atlas/archer-collection.jpg)

### Mage

Line up your targets and send a bolt through the whole rank. Save mana for the next rush. Find **Lady Elyndra** in Aethelgard for weapons and armor.

![Mage weapons and all twelve armor pieces](docs/atlas/mage-collection.jpg)

## Defend the sanctuary

The wave display tracks enemies in the field and incoming reinforcements. Enemies arrive in waves. Scouts flank, brutes push toward the center, and enemy archers try to keep their distance. Watch their attack warnings, protect the sanctuary, and leave yourself enough room to dodge.

Return to your class mentor in Aethelgard to forge weapons and individual armor pieces. Goran equips Warriors, Lady Elyndra equips Mages, and Diana equips Archers. Mix your gear and compare its power or defense before equipping it.

Torvald sells potions and permanent caravan upgrades in Market Square. Rescue six stranded citizens across the campaign and watch them settle into the city. Select a returned citizen in the rescue ledger and choose **Find in City** to follow their marker through the streets. Boss victories award XP and crystals; new equipment comes from your mentor.

![Actual gameplay: a Mage defending the meadow sanctuary with a focused enemy's attack timer visible](docs/images/player-target-focus.png)

### On the battlefield

Enemy archers and hexers telegraph shots at the Beacon and at survivors. Step into the firing line, face the attacker, and parry to earn a riposte. If a survivor falls behind, hold Interact when their rescue card offers the call.

## Follow the northern road

Travel from **Ember Meadow** through the **Ash Cemetery** and **Frozen Pass** to the **Hollow Necropolis**. Face the Ash Warden and Hollow King in dedicated boss chapters. They use rushes, slams, shockwaves, and ground spells; their attacks become more dangerous below half health.

In solo play, the **World Map / Chapters** screen lets you return to unlocked chapters with your current gear. Travelling restarts that chapter and replaces its checkpoint.

![The four campaign maps](docs/atlas/route-a5030363.jpg)

## Start playing

Download the archive for your computer from the [latest release](https://github.com/Chris020606/JavaGame/releases/latest), extract the whole folder, and open **Outpost**. Java is included in the desktop downloads.

Music and sound effects have separate volume controls in **Settings**.

On Mac, choose **macOS ARM64** for Apple Silicon or **macOS x64** for Intel. Keep the application and its bundled files together.

<summary><strong>Default controls</strong></summary>

| Action | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Move | W A S D |
| Aim and attack | Left mouse button |
| Attack toward your facing | Space |
| Charge a heavy attack | Hold Q, then release |
| Parry | F |
| Lunge | R |
| Dodge / sprint | Ctrl / Shift |
| Heal with a potion | H |
| Interact / sanctuary blessing | G |
| Focus an enemy | T |
| Inventory | E |
| Character stats inside inventory | C |
| Recall to city between fights | B |
| Craft equipment | Talk to your class mentor in the city |
| Orbit the camera | Hold right mouse button |
| Pause | Esc |

Controls can be changed in Settings. The sanctuary's marked ring restores health. Stand between an enemy archer and the beacon or a survivor, face the incoming shot, and time a parry to intercept it and ready a riposte. When the beacon takes a hit, its marker pulses red on the radar. Press G inside the ring to repair up to 100 integrity for 30 crystals. Smaller repairs cost proportionally less.
