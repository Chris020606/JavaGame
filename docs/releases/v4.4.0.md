# Outpost v4.4.0 — Aethelgard

Your watch begins inside the city. Meet your mentor, receive your first weapon, and venture beyond the gates to bring the missing home.

- Explore Aethelgard's market, class workshops, residential lanes and castle district.
- Follow a new story opening and split city/combat tutorial.
- Rescue six citizens with distinct appearances that carry over from the field to their city homes.
- Browse the redesigned inventory, compare equipment, and learn your stats through interactive cards.
- Forge helmets, chestplates, greaves and boots individually with your class mentor.
- Buy provisions and permanent upgrades from Torvald's caravan.
- Face enemies with improved flanking, spacing and ranged retreat behavior.
- Find city services with the circular radar and nearby NPC labels.
- Track returned citizens and read their stories in the rescue ledger.

Bosses award XP and crystals rather than free equipment. Later character levels grow more steadily, and higher-tier forging costs more. Existing owned equipment is preserved; older character health adjusts to the revised maximum.

Two-player co-op remains available through LAN or a private VPN, with separate campaign checkpoints. The new city and mentor progression are part of the solo adventure.

Validation includes 173 unit tests, native city/inventory checks, field-survivor rendering and desktop packaging. The complete historical native input campaign has not been rerun against every new screen; desktop CI verifies builds across the four supported platforms.
