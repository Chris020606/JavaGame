# Outpost v4.5.0 — The Road Home

Aethelgard has a new welcome: snowy peaks, drifting clouds, woodland, and a river winding through the valley. The title sky moves from night to day; grab the sun or moon to set the hour yourself.

## Around the citadel

Talk with your mentors and returned citizens through clearer conversation panels. NPCs turn toward you naturally and share the city's lighting and shadows. Track a destination from the city radar, compare equipment, or filter your collection to see owned upgrades.

## Beyond the gates

The Ash Warden and Hollow King have stronger phase patterns: eruption lines, staggered shockwaves, and an open-sector crown of sigils. Watch the warnings and find your escape route. Easy keeps more generous warning times and fewer overlapping attacks.

Survivor encounters remain available when the last enemy falls. Returning to the city after a chapter no longer repeats its completion screen when you deploy again. Citizens use the same distinct models in the field and at home.

## A clearer fight

The wave display distinguishes enemies in the field, incoming reinforcements, and rescues awaiting extraction. Kill rewards use a compact combined label. Enemy names and attack descriptions fit their target frames.

Music and effects have independent saved volume controls. Inactive music streams stop after crossfades, and crowd impacts no longer repeatedly restart the same sound.

## Single-player focus

Multiplayer menus, hosting, joining, and networking have been removed. Existing single-player saves remain compatible. Boss rewards are XP and crystals; equipment comes from your mentor, piece by piece.

The opening story is shorter, and chapter story panels no longer cover combat. Full chapter lore remains in the journal.
