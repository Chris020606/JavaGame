# Third-party components

Keep these notices and the included license files when sharing builds.

- **Jaylib 6.0.1-0**, Electron Studio: GPLv3 with the Classpath Exception, as supplied in its `REAL-LICENSE.txt`. Copy: `licenses/Jaylib.txt`. Source, native build scripts, and submodule revisions: https://github.com/electronstudio/jaylib and https://github.com/electronstudio/jaylib/releases.
- **JavaCPP 1.5.11**, Bytedeco: Apache 2.0 / GPLv2 with Classpath Exception terms as supplied by the project. Copy: `licenses/JavaCPP.txt`. Source: https://github.com/bytedeco/javacpp/tree/1.5.11.
- **raylib**, Ray and contributors: zlib/libpng license, `licenses/raylib.txt`. Jaylib's raylib submodule fixes the source revision: https://github.com/electronstudio/jaylib/tree/master/raylib.
- **SDL**, Sam Lantinga and contributors: zlib license, `licenses/SDL.txt`. Source: https://github.com/libsdl-org/SDL. Native versions are bundled by Jaylib.
- **KayKit Adventurers 2.0 FREE** and **KayKit Character Animations 1.1**, Kay Lousberg: CC0. Original license files remain inside their resource folders. The derived raider, archer, and shaman models remap bundled animations by bone name. https://kaylousberg.com/
- **Nature Kit (2.1)**, Kenney: CC0. Selected unmodified OBJ/MTL models are included in `resources/outpost/nature`, with the original `License.txt` and a source/checksum manifest. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/nature-kit ; creator page: https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit .
- **Rajdhani Medium**, Indian Type Foundry: SIL Open Font License 1.1. Font and original license are bundled as `resources/outpost/interface.ttf` and `interface-LICENSE.txt`. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/rajdhani .
- Portable native releases include a **Java runtime**. Its license and notices remain in the runtime's `legal` directory. CI builds use Eclipse Temurin: https://adoptium.net/ and https://github.com/adoptium/temurin25-binaries.

The outpost, crates, paths, and lanterns are original project scenery; vegetation, rocks, tents, and ruin props use the Kenney pack above. Synthesized audio is generated by the scripts in `tools/`. Downloaded, unused third-party archives are not included in game packages.
